# Errata to FTEF 0.1.0

These corrections apply to FTEF 0.1.0 as tagged (v0.1.0). They change how valid records are evaluated and
nothing that validates: every record and policy valid under 0.1.0 stays valid. The reference evaluator and its
vectors (tools/errata_vectors.py) implement them. Security advisory: GHSA-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx (filled in on publication).

## Corrections
- Adverse evidence (6.1, 6.2): the situation-level and state freshness scales also shortened how long an adverse
  item stays in play, so raising the situation level or moving a partner down could restore trust an adverse item
  had cancelled. Corrected: adverse items stay in play for twice their unscaled class freshness; defects in an
  adverse item never take it out of play; an adverse item cancels every item whose coverage it may overlap.
- Coverage (5.2, 6.1): coverage was compared as an exact string. Corrected: compared after normalisation, and
  overlapping unless the relying party has recorded it as distinct.
- Evidence classes (5.3, 6.1): an item could count at a class whose required axes it no longer met after its binding
  was verified. Corrected: an item counts no higher than its claimed class nor than the best class whose axes it meets.
- Decisions (6.6): a decision's notAfter was judged against a time inside the decision itself; resolution was missing
  from the list of decision purposes. Corrected: judged against the relying party's own application time, with
  nonces kept until notAfter plus skew; resolution listed (decided by the admission roles in 0.1).
- Acceptances and limits (6.3, 6.5): the evidence that justifies an acceptance did not have to count when it was
  decided; conflicting limits had no rule. Corrected: it must have counted at the decision; the strictest limit of
  each type applies, whenever an acceptance is active.
- Status (8.1, 6.4): unknown status values had no meaning; expired trust marks had no stated effect. Corrected:
  unknown values mean suspended and are never restored automatically; an expired mark voids its supporting evidence
  while its adverse evidence keeps cancelling; a status token dated beyond the clock skew does not count.
- Ladder (5.6): a policy could give lower states higher caps or longer freshness. Corrected: clamped at evaluation.

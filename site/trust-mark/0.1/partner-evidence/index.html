Trust mark type: partner evidence

https://ftef.vdhome.be/trust-mark/0.1/partner-evidence is the trust_mark_type of FTEF profile 8.1: an OpenID Federation trust mark, issued by a registry about a federation partner, that carries evidence summaries and a Token Status List reference for the partner's state. It never carries a trust band.

Definition: section 8.1 of the specification.
